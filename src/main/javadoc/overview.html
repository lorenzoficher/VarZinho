VarZinho captures highlights in a sports gym: every camera keeps its last 30 seconds in a circular buffer, and pressing a court's button turns that window into a permanent clip.

It is the project for AL0330, Object-Oriented Programming, at Unipampa Alegrete. Its focus is the domain model, not video processing: fixed and PTZ cameras carry metadata only, and a phone's real .mp4 clips are written by FFmpeg, an external program, behind an interface. No frame is ever decoded in Java.

How a capture happens

  1. A clock hands every court one second of footage, and each recording {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.capture.Camera} writes it into its {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.capture.CircularBuffer}, overwriting the oldest second.
  2. The operator presses a court's button. {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.application.CaptureHighlight} asks the {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.structure.Court}, whose first recording camera freezes its last 30 seconds into a {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.highlight.VideoClip}, and the court stamps the clip as an immutable {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.highlight.Highlight}.
  3. The use case returns it as a {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.application.PendingCapture}, whose {@code archive()} stores it through {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.application.port.HighlightRepository}, in CSV files or in PostgreSQL.

Layers

The packages follow Clean Architecture. Each layer depends only on the layers inside it, and ArchitectureTest fails the build on any import that points outward.

Layers, from the innermost out
LayerPackagesHolds
Domain {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.structure structure}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.capture capture}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.highlight highlight}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.domain.people people}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.enums enums}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.exception exception} The entities and their rules. Imports nothing from the other layers, nor Swing, SQL or FFmpeg.
Application {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.application application}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.application.port application.port} The use cases, the read models the window shows, and the storage contracts (ports).
Infrastructure {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.infrastructure.persistence persistence}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.infrastructure.stream stream}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.infrastructure.simulation simulation}, {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.infrastructure.spectator spectator} The adapters: CSV, PostgreSQL and in-memory storage, FFmpeg, the simulated clock and the public spectator view.
Presentation {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.ui ui} The Swing window. Reaches the model only through the use cases and owns no business rule.
Composition root {@link br.edu.unipampa.varzinho.bootstrap bootstrap} The one place that names every concrete adapter and puts the application together.
